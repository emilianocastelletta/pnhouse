PN HOUSE is a welcoming apartment in San Gemini, designed for guests who want to explore Umbria at their own pace. Bright interiors combine modern lines with exposed wooden beams.

The 50 m² apartment has one double bedroom, a living area with a sofa bed, an equipped kitchen, a bathroom, a balcony and a terrace. It accommodates up to four guests.
